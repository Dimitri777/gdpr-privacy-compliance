## How to use

1. Complete the **Audit Checklist**.
2. Fill the **Record of Processing Activities**.
3. Adapt the Privacy Policy and procedures to your processing.
4. Put DPAs in place with all processors.
5. Train staff and keep evidence.

> **Disclaimer:** Templates are educational / starting points only. They do not constitute legal advice. Always adapt to your specific processing and seek qualified counsel where needed.
