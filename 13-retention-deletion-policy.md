# Data Retention and Deletion Policy

**Organisation:** [Name]  
**Effective date:** [Date]  
**Last reviewed:** [Date]

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out the retention periods for personal data processed by the organisation and the procedures for secure deletion or anonymisation once the retention period expires or the data is no longer required.

## 2. Principles

- Personal data shall be kept in a form which permits identification of data subjects for no longer than is necessary for the purposes for which the data are processed (storage limitation).
- Retention periods are defined by purpose, legal/regulatory requirements and business need.
- When retention expires, data shall be securely deleted or irreversibly anonymised.

## 3. Retention schedule (examples – adapt to your processing)

| Category of data | Purpose | Retention period | Trigger for deletion |
|------------------|---------|------------------|----------------------|
| Customer account data | Contract performance | Duration of contract + 6 years | End of contract / last activity |
| Marketing consents & contact lists | Direct marketing | Until withdrawal of consent + short suppression period | Withdrawal / objection |
| Employee HR records | Employment | Duration of employment + statutory period (e.g. 6 years) | End of employment |
| Applicant data (not hired) | Recruitment | 6–12 months | End of recruitment process |
| Website logs / analytics | Security & improvement | 12–26 months | Collection date |
| CCTV footage | Security | 30 days (unless incident) | Recording date |
| Support tickets | Customer support | 3 years after closure | Ticket closure |

## 4. Deletion procedure

1. Identify data that has reached the end of its retention period (periodic review or automated jobs).
2. Verify that no legal hold or ongoing dispute requires continued retention.
3. Securely delete or anonymise the data (including backups where feasible).
4. Record the deletion (date, categories, method, responsible person).
5. Update RoPA / inventories if necessary.

## 5. Data subject erasure requests

Erasure requests under Art. 17 GDPR are handled according to the DSAR procedure. Where erasure is not required (e.g. legal obligation to retain), the data subject is informed.

## 6. Responsibilities

- Data owners / process owners: define and maintain retention periods for their processing.
- IT / Security: implement technical deletion and ensure backups are covered.
- DPO / Privacy team: monitor compliance and advise.
