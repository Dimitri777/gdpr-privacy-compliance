# Personal Data Breach Response Plan

## 1. Purpose

This plan sets out the steps to be taken when a personal data breach is suspected or confirmed, in accordance with Articles 33 and 34 GDPR.

## 2. What is a personal data breach?

A breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure of, or access to, personal data.

## 3. Immediate actions (first hours)

1. Contain the incident (isolate systems, revoke access, stop ongoing leakage).
2. Preserve evidence (logs, forensic images).
3. Notify the internal incident lead / DPO / management immediately.
4. Begin assessment of risk to individuals.

## 4. Notification to Supervisory Authority (Art. 33)

**Deadline: without undue delay and, where feasible, not later than 72 hours after becoming aware.**

Notification must include:
- Nature of the breach
- Categories and approximate number of data subjects and records
- Contact details of DPO or other contact point
- Likely consequences
- Measures taken or proposed to address the breach and mitigate effects

If notification cannot be made within 72 hours, reasons for the delay must be provided.

## 5. Notification to data subjects (Art. 34)

Required **without undue delay** when the breach is likely to result in a **high risk** to the rights and freedoms of natural persons.

Communication should describe the nature of the breach and recommendations for the individuals concerned.

Exceptions exist where:
- Appropriate technical measures (e.g. encryption) were applied, or
- Subsequent measures ensure high risk is no longer likely, or
- Individual notification would involve disproportionate effort (public communication may be used instead).

## 6. Internal register

All breaches (even those not notified) must be documented, including facts, effects and remedial action taken.

## 7. Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| Any staff member | Report suspected breach immediately |
| Incident lead / DPO | Coordinate response, risk assessment, notifications |
| IT / Security | Containment, forensics, technical remediation |
| Management | Decision-making, resource allocation, external communications |
