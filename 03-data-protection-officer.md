# Data Protection Officer (DPO) – Appointment & Contact

## 1. When a DPO is required (Art. 37 GDPR)

A Data Protection Officer must be designated when:
- the processing is carried out by a public authority or body (except courts acting in their judicial capacity);
- the core activities consist of processing operations which require regular and systematic monitoring of data subjects on a large scale; or
- the core activities consist of processing on a large scale of special categories of data (Art. 9) or personal data relating to criminal convictions and offences (Art. 10).

Even when not mandatory, appointing a DPO is considered good practice.

## 3. Tasks of the DPO (Art. 39)

- Inform and advise the controller/processor and employees of their GDPR obligations
- Monitor compliance with the GDPR and with the organisation’s policies
- Provide advice on Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs)
- Cooperate with the supervisory authority
- Act as the contact point for the supervisory authority and for data subjects

## 4. Publication

The contact details of the DPO must be published and communicated to the relevant supervisory authority.

**Published contact:** [email / web page URL]

## 5. Independence & resources

The DPO shall not receive any instructions regarding the exercise of their tasks, shall report to the highest management level, and shall be provided with the resources necessary to carry out their tasks.
