# Data Subject Access Request (DSAR) and Rights Procedure

## 1. Scope

Procedure for handling requests under Chapter III GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, withdrawal of consent).

## 2. How requests may be submitted

- Email: [privacy@example.com]
- Postal address: [address]
- Web form / portal (if available)

## 3. Identity verification

Reasonable steps must be taken to verify the identity of the requester before disclosing personal data. Additional information may be requested where necessary.

## 4. Time limits

- Respond without undue delay and at the latest within **one month** of receipt.
- Extension by a further two months is possible for complex or numerous requests; the data subject must be informed within the first month, with reasons.

## 5. Steps

1. Log the request and date of receipt.
2. Verify identity.
3. Locate all relevant personal data.
4. Assess any exemptions or restrictions that may apply.
5. Prepare the response (in a commonly used electronic form where requested).
6. Provide the information or explain why the request is refused.
7. Record the outcome and retention of the request handling records.

## 6. Fees

Information and actions are provided free of charge. A reasonable fee may be charged (or the request refused) only where requests are manifestly unfounded or excessive.
