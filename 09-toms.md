# Technical and Organisational Measures (TOMs)  
Article 32 GDPR

**Organisation:** [Name]  
**Last reviewed:** [Date]

## 1. Pseudonymisation and encryption
- Encryption of personal data at rest and in transit (TLS 1.2+)
- Pseudonymisation where feasible for analytics / testing

## 2. Confidentiality
- Role-based access control (RBAC) and least-privilege principle
- Multi-factor authentication for privileged accounts
- Confidentiality agreements / NDAs with staff and contractors
- Secure disposal of media and paper

## 3. Integrity
- Input validation and integrity checks
- Versioning and change control for critical systems
- Anti-malware and vulnerability management

## 4. Availability and resilience
- Regular backed-up data with tested restore procedures
- Redundancy / high-availability architecture where appropriate
- Business continuity and disaster recovery plans
- DDoS protection

## 5. Ability to restore availability and access after an incident
- Documented incident response and recovery procedures
- RTO / RPO targets defined and tested

## 6. Processes for regular testing, assessment and evaluation
- Periodic vulnerability scanning and penetration testing
- Security awareness training for staff
- Regular review of access rights
- Audit logging and monitoring of privileged activity
- Periodic review of this TOMs document

## 7. Physical security
- Controlled access to premises and server rooms
- Visitor logs and CCTV where appropriate

---

These measures are selected taking into account the state of the art, implementation costs, and the nature, scope, context and purposes of processing as well as the risks to the rights and freedoms of natural persons.
