# International Data Transfers  
Chapter V GDPR

## 1. Principle

Personal data may only be transferred to a third country or international organisation if the conditions of Chapter V are met, ensuring that the level of protection is not undermined.

## 3. Transfer Impact Assessment (TIA)

Where SCCs (or similar) are used and the destination country does not provide essentially equivalent protection, a Transfer Impact Assessment must be performed and documented. Key points to assess:

- Laws and practices of the third country (especially government access)
- Supplementary measures (technical, contractual, organisational)
- Residual risk and decision to proceed / not proceed

## 4. Supplementary measures (examples)

- Encryption with keys controlled by the exporter / EEA entity
- Pseudonymisation
- Split processing
- Contractual obligations on the importer regarding transparency and challenge of requests
- Organisational policies on handling government access requests

## 5. Record keeping

- Keep copies of SCCs / other transfer tools
- Keep TIAs and related risk assessments
- Keep records of sub-processors located outside the EEA
- Review transfers periodically and upon material changes
